About us

Property ownership should be clear and within reach

MKUYU Africa is a Tanzanian property development, sales and rental company, founded and led by Juneid Othman. We build, sell and manage homes in Tanzania.

Our ambition is deliberately large: to construct one million modern, affordable homes across Tanzania, widening access to quality housing through affordable ownership options and instalment payment plans rather than insisting on a single upfront payment.

We also believe the boring parts matter most. Ownership documents, contract terms, project location and payment conditions should be explained plainly before anyone commits money — and a broker should always be identifiable.

Miliki Ardhi Diaspora

Our programme for Tanzanians living abroad enables them to begin owning property in Tanzania while still overseas, through structured and professionally managed arrangements. It exists because informal arrangements — sending money home to relatives — have too often ended in dispute and lost investment.

Ask about the programme

Professional brokerage

Through the Tanzania Brokers Association, where our founder serves on the board, the aim is to professionalise the brokerage industry so clients can tell a qualified broker from a fraudster — starting with asking for official identification.
